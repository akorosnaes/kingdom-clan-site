About Us

About Kingdom

Kingdom CC is a social clan with a focus on PvM and events. We aim to create a family-like atmosphere where trust and communication are at the forefront. We also pride ourselves on our event variety intended to keep the game fresh and competitive.

Core Values

  • Family: Families respect each other, praise their achievements and move past their failures.
  • Fun: We hope to improve each player's experience while playing the game by creating a welcoming atmosphere with regular events.
  • Trust/Teamwork: Trusting in each other leads to a healthier, more respectful family, regardless of rank/stature. We have each other's back.
  • Communication: Transparency in the ranking system, bios for ranks, feedback addressed directly.
