Who we are

Kingdom is a social clan with a focus on PvM and events. We aim to create a family-like atmosphere where trust and communication are at the forefront. We also pride ourselves on our event variety intended to keep the game fresh and competitive.

Core values
  • Family Families respect each other, praise their achievements and move past their failures.
  • Fun We hope to improve each player's experience while playing the game by creating a welcoming atmosphere with regular events.
  • Trust & teamwork Trusting in each other leads to a healthier, more respectful family, regardless of rank/stature. We have each other's back.
  • Communication Transparency in the ranking system, bios for ranks, feedback addressed directly.

Your trial

One month as a Trialist

We are excited to have you in the clan and look forward to getting to know you. An important part of the culture in this clan is voice chatting, in-game chatting, participation in events and joining for activities. Our hope is that by joining this community you are no longer playing isolated and instead know and can hang out with others.

As you do content with others, ask them to post anonymous feedback in your trial thread.

Trialist expectations
  • Activity Be active in voice/clan chat and Discord. Join in on voice chat, events, or group content when you can.
  • Attitude Be friendly and respectful. We want to add members who contribute and bring good vibes, not just numbers.
  • Participation Show up for at least one clan event, requiring voice chat.
  • Trial length & feedback One month. During this month, members will provide feedback. Admins use feedback to pass trialists to the Clan Mate rank. Please take it upon yourself to meet members; no feedback after one month will not be sufficient.
